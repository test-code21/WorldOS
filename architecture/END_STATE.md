# End State — What WorldOS 1.0 Should Feel Like

WorldOS 1.0 should feel like a **quiet piece of infrastructure**.

An operator can deploy it and say:

> These are the public sources I care to observe. Preserve what they communicate, tell me when their observed content changes, let me search the historical record, and expose it to my other software without inventing conclusions for me.

## Mature capabilities

- curated source catalog;
- durable observations;
- immutable historical versions;
- addressable document segments;
- mechanical processing lineage;
- longitudinal diffs;
- provenance-preserved search;
- stable external API;
- global reference coverage dashboard;
- community catalog/federation bundles;
- reliable self-hosting;
- GCP reference deployment;
- clean handoff to external intelligence systems.

## What success is not

Success is not "WorldOS knows everything."

Success is:

> WorldOS can make a growing, transparent slice of the public information environment durable and computationally accessible without pretending that preservation itself is judgment.
